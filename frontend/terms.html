Terms and acceptable use

Using Saple responsibly

Saple is a student project built for a database course. These terms describe what it is, what it is not, and what is expected of the people using it.

Saple is an independent BUET CSE academic project for company and career insights. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official login or careers service for any company named on the site.

What Saple is

Saple is an independent academic project produced for a BUET CSE database course. It demonstrates a relational design for company insights, salary context, reviews, interview experiences, employment verification and job postings.

It is not a commercial service, not a recruitment agency, and not the official careers or login site of any company named on it. Much of the data in a demonstration deployment is synthetic and is labelled as such.

Accounts

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you submit and for keeping your password to yourself.

Public registration can only ever create an ordinary job-seeker or employee account. Administrator and company representative roles are granted by an administrator and can be revoked at any time.

Contributing honestly

When you submit a salary figure, a review or an interview experience:

  • Describe your own experience, not someone else’s.
  • Do not name individual colleagues or managers, or include anyone’s personal contact details.
  • Do not submit confidential material you are not free to share.
  • Do not submit content intended to defame, harass or mislead.
  • Expect every contribution to be reviewed before it is published, and to be removed if it breaks these rules.

Applying to a vacancy

One application per vacancy. Applications are read by the approved representatives of that company and by administrators where oversight is needed.

Do not put identity documents, national ID numbers, bank details or passwords into an application statement. Saple neither needs nor wants them.

Representing a company

A company representative acts only for the companies assigned to their account. Using the workspace to reach another company’s data, or to retaliate against a contributor, ends the assignment.

Vacancies must describe real roles at the assigned company and must not be used for advertising, recruitment spam or data collection.

Things that are not allowed

Regardless of account type:

  • Automated scraping, bulk downloading or load testing without the project owner’s permission.
  • Attempting to bypass verification, moderation or the company scope rules.
  • Uploading or linking to malware, phishing pages or deceptive content.
  • Impersonating a company, a Saple administrator, or anyone else.
  • Using Saple to collect credentials or payment details from other people.

No warranty

Saple is provided as an academic demonstration, without warranty. Salary ranges are aggregates of contributed data, not official statements from any employer, and should not be relied on for a financial or legal decision.

The project may be taken offline, reset or changed at any time as part of coursework.

Reporting a problem

Every public contribution can be reported from the page it appears on. Reports go to the administrator queue and each one is triaged and resolved with a recorded decision.

For security problems, see the Security page instead.
