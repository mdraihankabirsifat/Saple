About the project

Career context, modeled responsibly

Saple is a database-focused academic platform inspired by company-review and salary-insight services. It helps freshers and job seekers explore companies, salary information, employee reviews, interview experiences, and supported benefits.

The problem

Scattered career information

Early-career job seekers often compare employers using incomplete or disconnected sources. Saple organizes common decision signals while keeping their data origin and limitations visible.

How it works

Browse, contribute, review

Public visitors browse approved records. Eligible verified employees submit structured salary, review, or interview information. Administrators review pending requests and contributions before public publication.

Verification and moderation

Separate trust checks

Employment verification grants company-specific contribution access; it does not publish evidence. Moderation separately determines whether a contribution is approved, rejected, or flagged, with an internal audit history. The current build does not enforce designation-specific verification.

Salary context

Verified and community ranges

The Verified Salary Range uses approved records with verified contributor status. The Community Salary Range uses all approved salary records under the current query, including verified and unverified records. Counts matter: neither range is an official employer statement.

Privacy

Responsible contribution

Public endpoints omit user IDs, emails, verification evidence, reporter identities, and moderation internals. Anonymous display can hide a public author name while internal ownership remains available for authorization and accountability.

Technology

A database-first stack

  • Oracle 19c
  • Node.js and Express
  • node-oracledb Thin mode
  • Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • JWT authentication

Academic-project disclaimer

Demonstration, not an employer publication

Saple is an academic database project. It may include synthetic demonstration data to exercise queries, filtering, aggregation, relationships, verification, and moderation. Demo salaries and ratings must not be treated as official company statements. Saple does not claim official affiliation with any listed company.

Future scope

Practical next steps

Possible future improvements include deployment, stronger verified email workflows and delivery monitoring, designation-scoped verification, expanded moderation tools, continued accessibility improvements, and the proposed minimal ML decision-support extension. These items are not presented as operational. Any ML support would remain advisory, require at least 50 moderator-reviewed historical records for a role before activation, and never replace a moderator.
