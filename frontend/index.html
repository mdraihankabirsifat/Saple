Trust by design

Every number on Saple says where it came from

Three rules decide what you see. They are enforced in the database and the API, not just in the wording on a page.

01

Approved-only publication

Every contribution starts as PENDING and is invisible to the public. It appears only after an administrator sets it to APPROVED, and the decision is recorded with the actor, the previous state, the new state and a timestamp.

02

Verified vs Community ranges

The Verified Salary Range uses only approved submissions from contributors who passed employment verification for that exact company and role. The Community Salary Range uses every approved submission. Both always show their contribution count.

03

Anonymous publicly, accountable internally

A contribution can be shown without its author. Saple still keeps the internal link between the record and the account, so a report can be investigated and a moderation decision can be audited afterwards.

    How it works

    Three steps, whichever side you are on

    1. 1

      Discover a company

      Search the directory by name, industry, city or country. Each profile carries benefits, reviews and both salary ranges side by side.

    2. 2

      Read trusted insights

      Compare the verified range against the community range, read approved reviews and interview experiences, and report anything that looks wrong.

    3. 3

      Verify, contribute or apply

      Verify your employment for one exact company and role to contribute, or apply to an open vacancy and track the decision from your own dashboard.

    Who Saple is for

    Four roles, four different views

    Job seeker

    Browse companies, salary ranges, reviews and interviews. Apply to open vacancies and track every application privately.

    Create a free account →

    Current or former employee

    Verify your employment for one exact company and role, then contribute salary, review and interview data for that scope — anonymously if you choose.

    How verification works →

    Company representative

    Granted by an administrator, never by signing up. Review verification requests, publish vacancies and manage applications for your assigned companies only.

    Request an assignment →

    Administrator

    Moderates contributions, triages reports, approves and revokes representative assignments, and publishes announcements. There can be many administrators; none is hard-coded.

    Administrator access is assigned internally and is never available through public registration.

    Safety and transparency

    How this project handles your data and your reports

    Saple loads no third-party scripts, trackers or advertising, stores passwords and reset links only as hashes, and publishes exactly what it collects. Every public contribution can be reported, and every report is triaged with a recorded decision.
